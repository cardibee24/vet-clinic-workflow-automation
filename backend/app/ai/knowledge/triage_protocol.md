# Triage Protocol

Use this protocol to sort walk-ins and phone calls into urgency tiers before they reach the check queue.

## Tier 1 - Immediate (see within 5 minutes)
- Difficulty breathing or open-mouth breathing in a cat
- Active, uncontrolled bleeding
- Suspected poisoning or ingestion of a toxic substance
- Collapse, seizure, or unresponsiveness
- Bloated, distended abdomen with unproductive retching (suspected GDV)
- Hit by car or other major trauma in the last hour

## Tier 2 - Urgent (see within 30 minutes)
- Vomiting or diarrhea more than 3 times in the last 24 hours
- Limping and unwilling to bear weight on a limb
- Straining to urinate with little or no output, especially in male cats
- Eye injury or sudden onset of squinting
- Known allergic reaction (facial swelling, hives) without breathing difficulty

## Tier 3 - Routine (see within normal queue order)
- Mild itching or a single instance of vomiting with normal energy and appetite
- Follow-up recheck for a healing wound
- Nail trim, anal gland expression, or other maintenance requests
- Vaccine due reminders

If a description matches more than one tier, always assign the higher (more urgent) tier. When in doubt, escalate rather than downgrade.
