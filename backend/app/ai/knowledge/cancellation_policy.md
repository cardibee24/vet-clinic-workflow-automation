# Appointment Cancellation Policy

- Owners may cancel or reschedule an appointment free of charge if they notify the clinic at least 4 hours before the scheduled time.
- Cancellations made less than 4 hours before the appointment, or no-shows, are subject to a $35 late-cancellation fee added to the owner's account.
- The late-cancellation fee is waived once per owner per rolling 12-month period as a courtesy - check the owner's history before charging it.
- Surgical and dental procedures require 24 hours' notice to cancel or reschedule without a $75 fee, since the surgical suite and staff time are reserved in advance.
- Emergency walk-ins are never charged a cancellation fee, since no appointment slot was reserved.
- If a fee applies, add it to the owner's next invoice as a line item described "Late cancellation fee" rather than collecting cash at the desk.
