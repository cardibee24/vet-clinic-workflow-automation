# Walk-In Emergency Policy

- Walk-ins are always accepted during business hours; the clinic never turns away an animal in visible distress.
- On arrival, front desk staff should immediately apply the Triage Protocol to assign an urgency tier, before collecting any paperwork or payment information.
- Tier 1 patients are taken back immediately, even if it means pausing a routine appointment already in progress. Notify the attending vet verbally, don't wait for the queue system.
- Tier 2 and Tier 3 walk-ins are added to the check queue in triage order, not strictly arrival order - a Tier 2 walk-in goes ahead of a Tier 3 patient who arrived earlier.
- If the clinic is at capacity and cannot safely see a walk-in, staff should stabilize if possible and refer the owner to the nearest 24-hour emergency animal hospital; keep the referral hospital's phone number at the front desk at all times.
- After hours, the clinic's voicemail and website should direct owners with an emergency to the same 24-hour referral hospital.
