# Vaccination Schedule

## Puppies and kittens
- First visit: as early as 6-8 weeks old
- Core vaccines (DHPP for dogs, FVRCP for cats) repeated every 3-4 weeks until 16 weeks old
- Rabies vaccine given once the animal is at least 12 weeks old, per state law
- A booster for all core vaccines is due exactly one year after the final puppy/kitten dose

## Adult dogs
- DHPP booster every 3 years after the 1-year booster
- Rabies booster every 1 or 3 years depending on the vaccine brand used - check the certificate
- Bordetella (kennel cough) every 6-12 months for dogs that board, attend daycare, or visit dog parks
- Leptospirosis annually for dogs with outdoor or rural exposure

## Adult cats
- FVRCP booster every 3 years after the 1-year booster
- Rabies booster every 1 or 3 years depending on the vaccine brand used
- FeLV (feline leukemia) annually for cats with outdoor access or unknown FeLV status

## Notes for front desk
- Always check the patient's existing record for the vaccine brand before scheduling a rabies booster, since the interval depends on the brand.
- If an owner is unsure of prior vaccine history, treat the animal as unvaccinated and restart the initial series.
